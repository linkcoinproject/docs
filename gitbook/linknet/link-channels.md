# Link Channels

## 5.2 Link Channels

Link Channels extend payment channels beyond two parties.

### 5.2.1 Channel Factories

Open multiple channels with a single on-chain transaction:

*   3x reduction in on-chain transactions
*   Shared liquidity pool
*   Atomic multi-channel operations

### 5.2.2 Virtual Channels

Direct channels through intermediaries without trust. Using adaptor signatures, the virtual channel is cryptographically secured.

### 5.2.3 Linked Payments

Atomic multi-destination payments: either all complete or none complete.
